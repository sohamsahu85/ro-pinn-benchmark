Benchmarking a Physics-Informed Neural Network Against a Physics-Only
Regression and Gaussian Process Regression for Reverse Osmosis Membrane
Degradation Diagnosis

Soham Sahu, Aakarsh Agrawal, Dr. Rajesh Mahadeva
(Guided by Dr. Rajesh Mahadeva and Dr. Varadharajan)

- Under the paper's literal loss, the physics term has zero gradient on the network.
- PINN and plain NN are therefore bit-identical for lambda = 0, 1, 10, 100.
- A network-free physics-only regression is the best of 15 models on salinity.
- Physics-only gives +0.033 R2 (bootstrap-supported) and a 1.56x stronger anomaly signal.
- It reproduces the defect-fraction (beta) rise on degraded data with no network.
- GPR's predictive uncertainty is flat and fails to detect the degradation.
